Product design services

From building a new product to improving an existing experience, product design covers the key stages of turning business requirements and user needs into functional digital experiences.

01

New products and MVPs

Turn an early-stage idea into a structured product experience. Define core functionality, map essential user journeys and develop wireframes and interactive prototypes to prepare for validation and development.

02

Website and mobile app design

Create intuitive, responsive experiences for websites and mobile applications. From navigation and user flows to interface design and interactive prototypes, every element is structured around the needs of its users and the goals of the product.

03

SaaS and complex platforms

Simplify complex functionality across SaaS products, enterprise software and data-heavy platforms. This includes information architecture, dashboards, roles and permissions, navigation and workflows for products with multiple user types.

04

Product redesign and UX improvement

Identify usability issues, simplify existing workflows and improve information architecture. Redesign key product experiences to make functionality easier to understand, reduce friction and support users in completing their tasks.

05

AI-powered product design

Integrate AI functionality into digital products through clear, predictable and user-controlled interactions. This includes prompting, reviewing and editing generated content, handling errors and communicating uncertainty.

What product design includes

The scope depends on the product, its stage and the problems that need to be solved.

  • 01Product discovery and researchBusiness requirements, user needs, existing workflows and competitor analysis.
  • 02Information architecture and UX designProduct structure, navigation, user flows, edge cases and interface states.
  • 03Wireframes and prototypingConcepts and interactive prototypes for exploring and validating design decisions.
  • 04UI and interaction designResponsive interfaces, reusable components and detailed interaction patterns.
  • 05Design systemsComponent libraries, design patterns and documentation where appropriate.
  • 06Development handoffSpecifications, interaction details and collaboration with developers during implementation.

How the process works

  1. 01

    Understand

    Research the product, its users, business goals and constraints. Depending on the project, this stage may include stakeholder interviews, product analysis and competitor research.

  2. 02

    Structure

    Define requirements, organize information architecture and map key user journeys. Establish the product structure and prioritize the functionality needed to solve the core problem.

  3. 03

    Design and validate

    Develop wireframes, prototypes and high-fidelity interfaces. Test key flows where appropriate and refine the experience based on feedback.

  4. 04

    Prepare for development

    Document components, interactions, states and edge cases. Prepare implementation-ready designs and collaborate with developers to support delivery and subsequent improvements.

Frequently asked questions

What types of digital products can be designed?

Product design can cover websites, mobile applications, SaaS products, enterprise platforms, internal tools and AI-powered applications. The scope depends on the product's complexity, audience and business objectives.

Can an existing product be redesigned?

Yes. Product redesign can address usability issues, confusing navigation, inefficient workflows, inconsistent interfaces or changing business requirements. The work begins with understanding the current experience and identifying what needs to improve.

Can product design start from an idea?

Yes. Early-stage product design can include defining the core user journey, structuring functionality, creating wireframes and developing prototypes before full development begins.

What does a product design project include?

Deliverables may include research, information architecture, user flows, wireframes, prototypes, UI design, design system components and development specifications. The exact scope is defined according to the project.

How much do product design services cost?

The cost depends on the product's complexity, project scope, timeline and required deliverables. An estimate can be provided after the project requirements and expected outcomes are clarified.
