Design for the people who use it, and the business that runs it

A product can look good and still lose users, or lose money. I work on your product so that it works for both.

  • 01Users firstIf people cannot find what they need or understand what to do next, nothing else matters. I design around real tasks, not assumptions.
  • 02Business results in every decisionSign-ups, activation, purchases, retention. I ask what each screen should achieve for you, and design to that.
  • 03Conversion built in from the startI come from conversion research. I know where decisions break, so I design those moments with care instead of fixing them later.

What I design

Three kinds of work, each with a clear goal.

01

MVP and new product design

From a rough idea to a validated design your developers can build. We decide who it is for and what it must do first, and we test it before you spend on code.

Best for founders and teams building a first version.

02

SaaS and web platform design

Dashboards, admin tools and complex workflows made clear. I structure the information so people find what they need and act on it.

Best for data-heavy products that have outgrown their first design.

03

Redesign of an existing product

For live products where users get lost or drop off. I find the causes with evidence, then redesign the flows that cost you users and revenue.

Best for products with traffic but weak sign-up, activation or conversion.

How the work runs

Four steps, with regular reviews along the way. You always see where the work stands.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Goals, users, constraints and evidence. If your product is live, I use the same behavioral conversion diagnosis as in my CRO audits to see where people get lost.

  2. 02

    Structure

    Requirements, information architecture and the key user journeys. We agree what the product does first, and what waits.

  3. 03

    Design and validate

    Wireframes, a clickable prototype and the full interface. Weekly check-ins and reviews. Key flows are tested before development.

  4. 04

    Hand off and support

    Specs, states and edge cases for developers. I answer their questions during the build and check that what ships matches the design.

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of products do you design?

Mostly complex products: SaaS and B2B platforms, AI products, and data-heavy tools such as analytics portals. I also design MVPs, websites and landing pages. My case studies include a mobile app.

Can you start from an idea?

Yes. We define who the product is for and what it must do first, structure the key journeys, and build a clickable prototype. We test it with users before any development starts.

Can you redesign a product that is already live?

Yes. I first find out why users get lost or drop off, using the same behavioral conversion diagnosis as in my CRO audits. Then I redesign the flows that cost you users and revenue. If you want evidence before you commit, you can start with a CRO audit.

What does a project include?

It depends on the scope. Typically a clickable prototype, the full UI design, a design system or UI kit, and developer-ready specs. Research and validation are part of the work, with weekly check-ins and reviews.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions are included, as long as the scope does not change.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. After our first call I send you a plan with the steps and a timeline.

How much does a project cost?

The price depends on the scope, the timeline and the deliverables. As a guide, my rate is about $5,500 per month, or roughly $50 per hour. After the first call I send you a quote for your exact scope.

Do you work under NDA?

Yes. I keep client work confidential, which is why client projects are not shown on this page. I am glad to sign an NDA before we share product details.

Do you work with developers, and do you hand off?

Yes. I prepare specs, annotated flows, states and edge cases. During the build I answer developers’ questions and check that what ships matches the design.

What tools do you use?

I design in Figma and share the files with you and your developers.

Do you do development?

I do not work as a developer. I am an experienced Claude Code user, so I can share working HTML files as quick prototypes, and I can build a website or a platform with Claude Code. For anything that goes live, you need an experienced developer to check the code quality.

How do we get started?

Send me a request through the contact form, book an introductory call, or message me on LinkedIn. After the first call I send you a plan with the steps, a timeline and a quote for your scope.

What do you need from me to get started?

Access to your product, or a clear description of the idea. Access to the people who use it, or the data about them. One decision-maker on your side, and time from key stakeholders for a few sessions.

Can you join my team, or give a one-off review?

Yes. I can lead design on your team part-time, alongside your developers and product manager. I can also give focused advice in a short design review. We agree the format after the first call.
