How the work runs

Four steps, with regular reviews along the way. You always see where the work stands.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Goals, users, constraints and evidence. If your product is live, I use the same behavioral conversion diagnosis as in my CRO audits to see where people get lost.

  2. 02

    Structure

    Requirements, information architecture and the key user journeys. We agree what the product does first, and what waits.

  3. 03

    Design and validate

    Wireframes, a clickable prototype and the full interface. Weekly check-ins and reviews. Key flows are tested before development.

  4. 04

    Hand off and support

    Specs, states and edge cases for developers. I answer their questions during the build and check that what ships matches the design.
