Product design

Product design for SaaS and AI products

I'm a lead product designer with 20+ years of experience. I turn complex platforms, enterprise tools and AI features into products people can use, from the first idea to a build-ready interface.

Where I help most

Three kinds of work I take on, end to end or as one part of your team's process.

New products and MVPs

From idea to a clickable MVP: core flows, key screens and a prototype ready for testing and development.

Complex platforms and tools

Information architecture, roles and permissions, dashboards and workflows that stay clear as the product grows.

AI features

Flows for prompting, review, editing and regeneration that feel safe, understandable and under the user's control.

What you get

Depending on the project, the work includes:

  • User and stakeholder research, so decisions rest on how people really work
  • Requirements, information architecture and user flows, including edge cases and states
  • Wireframes and prototypes, tested with users before development
  • High-fidelity, component-based UI with the specs your developers need
  • A design system and light and dark modes when the product calls for them

How I work

The same four steps I used on products such as an internal asset platform and a data portal for the UN.

  1. UnderstandInterviews, analysis of the current tools and competitors, and a clear problem statement.
  2. StructureRequirements, MVP scope, information architecture and user flows.
  3. Design and testWireframes, prototypes and usability testing, then high-fidelity UI.
  4. Build and improveHandoff to the team and iterations after release.
