Terms

These terms cover the services I offer: a CRO audit and product design. They are written in plain language so you know what to expect before you start. By confirming the scope and paying the invoice, you agree to them.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

CRO audit

What you get

I audit the website or product experience you choose, in one of three scopes: a single page, a full conversion flow of up to six steps, or an e-commerce purchase journey. You receive a report with findings and the evidence behind each one, priorities in order, and a clear action plan with steps your team can follow. The report is delivered in Notion and/or as a PDF, together with a 20-minute Loom walkthrough of the findings.

Scope and price

The price is fixed and confirmed with you before work begins. The prices and estimates on this website are for planning and are not binding quotes. I confirm the final scope and price with you personally. Extras, such as covering both mobile and desktop for a flow, add-ons, or rush delivery, are agreed in the same way before you pay.

Turnaround

A single page audit is ready in 48 hours. A full conversion flow takes 3–5 business days, and an e-commerce purchase journey takes 4–6 business days. The work starts the day after your payment is received.

What I need from you

The address of the page or product, and access to anything that needs a login, such as a test account. Analytics access and session recordings are optional, but they make the findings stronger. I may ask a question or two during the audit, and the sooner I hear back, the sooner I can deliver.

What is not included

The audit is a diagnosis and an action plan. Implementation, meaning designing or building the fixes, is a separate piece of work. I do not run A/B tests as part of the audit. I may decline work that I consider unethical or harmful, and I will tell you why.

Refunds

If the audit does not meet your expectations, I will return your full payment, no questions asked.

Ownership

The report is yours. You can share it with your team and with anyone who works on your product.

Liability

My liability is limited to the amount you paid for the audit.

Product design

What you get

I design the product you choose, in the scope we agree: for example an MVP or new product, a SaaS or web platform, or a redesign of an existing product. Depending on the project, the work can include a clickable prototype, the full interface design, a design system or UI kit, and developer-ready specs. We can work as a fixed-scope project, as a short consulting engagement, or with me as a part-time lead designer on your team.

Scope, price and timeline

The price and the timeline depend on the scope and the deliverables. After our first call I send you a plan with the steps and a timeline, and a quote for your scope. As a guide, my rate is about $5,500 per month, or roughly $50 per hour. This guide is for planning and is not a binding quote. The final scope, price and timeline are agreed with you before work begins.

Revisions

Two rounds of revisions are included, as long as the scope does not change. A change to the scope is agreed with you before I work on it.

What I need from you

Access to your product, or a clear description of the idea. Access to the people who use it, or data about them. One decision-maker on your side, and time from key stakeholders for a few sessions. The sooner I hear back on questions, the sooner the work moves on.

What is not included

I design products; I do not work as a developer. I can share working HTML files as quick prototypes, and I can build a website or a platform with Claude Code. Code built this way needs to be checked by an experienced developer on your side before it goes live. I may decline work that I consider unethical or harmful, and I will tell you why.

Both services

Payment

You do not pay through this website. Once we agree on the scope and price, I send you an invoice, and the payment details are on it.

Confidentiality

I sign an NDA before work begins if you ask for one. What you share with me, including URLs, screenshots, analytics, product files and test accounts, is used only for your project and is kept for six months after the project is finished. I mention you or show your work in a case study only with your permission.

Results

I do not promise a specific business outcome, such as higher conversion or revenue, because results depend on many things outside my work, including how and when the recommendations and designs are carried out.

Changes to these terms

I may update these terms. The terms in force when you confirmed your order are the ones that apply to it. The date at the top shows when they were last changed.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, write to me through the contact form before you start, and I will answer.
