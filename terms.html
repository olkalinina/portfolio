Terms

These terms cover a CRO audit with me, Olga Kalinina. They are written in plain language so you know what to expect before you start. By confirming the scope and paying the invoice, you agree to them.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

What you get

I audit the website or product experience you choose, in one of three scopes: a single page, a full conversion flow of up to six steps, or an e-commerce purchase journey. You receive a report with findings and the evidence behind each one, priorities in order, and a clear action plan with steps your team can follow. The report is delivered in Notion and/or as a PDF, together with a 20-minute Loom walkthrough of the findings.

Scope and price

The price is fixed and confirmed with you before work begins. The prices and estimates on this website are for planning and are not binding quotes. I confirm the final scope and price with you personally. Extras, such as covering both mobile and desktop for a flow, add-ons, or rush delivery, are agreed in the same way before you pay.

Turnaround

A single page audit is ready in 48 hours. A full conversion flow takes 3–5 business days, and an e-commerce purchase journey takes 4–6 business days. The work starts the day after your payment is received.

What I need from you

The address of the page or product, and access to anything that needs a login, such as a test account. Analytics access and session recordings are optional, but they make the findings stronger. I may ask a question or two during the audit, and the sooner I hear back, the sooner I can deliver.

What is not included

The audit is a diagnosis and an action plan. Implementation, meaning designing or building the fixes, is a separate piece of work. I do not run A/B tests as part of the audit. I may decline work that I consider unethical or harmful, and I will tell you why.

Payment

You do not pay through this website. Once we agree on the scope and price, I send you an invoice, and the payment details are on it. Work starts the next day after the payment arrives.

Refunds

If the audit does not meet your expectations, I will return your full payment, no questions asked.

Ownership

The report is yours. You can share it with your team and with anyone who works on your product.

Confidentiality

I sign an NDA before work begins if you ask for one. What you share with me, including URLs, screenshots, analytics and test accounts, is used only for your audit and is kept for six months after the project is finished. I mention you or show your findings in a case study only with your permission.

Results

I do not promise a specific business outcome, such as higher conversion or revenue, because results depend on many things outside the audit, including how and when the recommendations are carried out. My liability is limited to the amount you paid for the audit.

Changes to these terms

I may update these terms. The terms in force when you confirmed your audit are the ones that apply to it. The date at the top shows when they were last changed.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, write to me through the contact form before you start, and I will answer.
