Understanding the audit

What is a CRO audit?

A CRO audit is a structured review of a website or digital product to identify barriers that may prevent visitors from completing a desired action. It examines the user journey, messaging, information structure, trust signals, and available behavioral evidence, then provides prioritized recommendations.

How is a CRO audit different from a UX audit?

A UX audit focuses on usability and the overall user experience. A CRO audit focuses on barriers to a specific outcome, such as a sign-up, purchase, or demo request. The two overlap, but a CRO audit keeps the conversion goal at the center of the diagnosis.

Read the full comparison in the CRO audit vs. UX audit article.

What’s included, and what isn’t?

Every audit includes:

  • Findings supported by evidence from your product
  • An explanation of each problem and why it matters
  • Prioritized recommendations and effort signals
  • Notes on confidence and limitations
  • A 20-minute Loom walkthrough explaining the findings, key problems, and how to address them

The audit provides diagnostic insights and direction. It does not include final copy, design files, implementation, ongoing optimization, or verification of results after changes are made.

I can recommend improvements to a page’s structure or information architecture, but the audit itself is not a redesign project.

What does the audit not promise?

The audit does not guarantee a conversion lift, forecast financial returns, or provide a guaranteed business outcome. It identifies and explains conversion barriers within the agreed scope, based on the available evidence.

It evaluates how your offer and pricing are presented, not your underlying pricing strategy, business model, or product-market fit.

What kinds of products do you work with?

I work with SaaS products, AI-powered products, digital platforms, marketplaces, e-commerce, and other digital services.

Does a CRO audit work for a product with low traffic?

Yes. A diagnosis can still identify messaging, trust, and usability problems. However, limited traffic restricts what analytics can reliably confirm. The report will distinguish observed issues from findings that cannot be fully validated with the available evidence.

Choosing your scope

How do I choose the right audit scope?

Single Page Audit examines one page, such as a homepage or product landing page.

Full Conversion Flow examines up to six steps in a user journey, from the initial touchpoint to a conversion goal. This could be a purchase journey or the decision-making journey for a SaaS product.

E-commerce Flow examines the shopping journey from the homepage to the completed purchase, including the key steps users take before buying.

If you’re unsure which scope fits, you can submit a request or book a free introductory call to discuss your product and goals.

Can you audit the experience inside my SaaS product?

Yes. An optional in-product audit is available as a separate paid add-on.

It examines the journey after sign-up, from entering the platform to completing the first key action that delivers value to the user. The goal is to identify friction that may prevent new users from experiencing the product’s core value.

This is not included in any standard package. Contact me to discuss adding it to your audit.

Can you review my marketing emails?

Yes. I offer an optional review of up to five marketing emails, including cold outreach, activation, and upgrade emails.

I assess how well the emails support your conversion journey: whether their messaging aligns with your website, addresses what users need to know, and encourages them to take the next step. I review the structure, content, calls to action, and consistency across the journey.

You’ll receive specific recommendations and suggested wording where improvements are needed. This service does not include email campaign analytics or performance metrics.

What do mobile and desktop coverage cost?

Every package includes a review of one device type: mobile or desktop.

You can add the second device for an additional 40% of the selected package price. Mobile and desktop are reviewed separately, and you receive two reports. This is a separate review of each experience, not just a responsiveness check.

What if part of my product is behind a login?

A test account is preferred so I can explore the product directly. If access isn’t possible, you can provide screenshots or screen recordings instead.

The report will note any limitations in what I could observe and how they affect the findings.

Process and delivery

What happens after I send a request?
  1. Scope review. I review your request and reply personally with the recommended scope and price before any payment.
  2. Confirmation and access. Once you agree on the scope and price, I send an invoice and request the access and materials needed for the audit.
  3. Audit and delivery. Work starts once payment and the necessary access and materials are received. I deliver the report within the agreed timeframe.
Is the introductory call free?

Yes. The call is free and comes with no obligation to proceed.

It’s an opportunity to meet me, discuss your product and the problems you’re facing, and explore what you’d like to improve. You can share your screen so we can look at your product together. We’ll discuss your needs and the appropriate audit scope, but I won’t conduct an analysis or provide recommendations during the call.

We can meet via Zoom or Google Meet. You don’t need to send your website in advance.

How long does an audit take?
  • Single Page Audit: 48 hours
  • Full Conversion Flow: 3–5 business days
  • E-commerce Flow: 4–6 business days

The timeline starts once payment is received and all necessary access and materials have been provided. Rush delivery is available for an additional fee.

What format will I receive my audit in?

The report format depends on the audit scope. A single-page audit is usually delivered as a PDF. For longer conversion flows, I may use Notion to organize each step, with screenshots and annotations highlighting specific issues and recommendations.

If both formats would be useful, I can provide both. If you don’t have a preference, I’ll choose the format that best presents the findings.

Is a call included with the audit?

Every package includes a 20-minute Loom video in which I walk you through the findings, explain the key problems affecting your product, and discuss how to address them. You can watch it at your own pace.

The audit is a standalone diagnostic deliverable. It includes prioritized recommendations and guidance on what to improve, but not design files, implementation, or iterative redesign work.

Do I need to provide analytics access?

No. Analytics tools such as GA4, Hotjar, or Microsoft Clarity can strengthen the diagnosis, but they’re not required. Without analytics, I examine the journey and product experience directly. Findings reflect the evidence available.

Will you tell me how much my conversion will rise?

No. I don’t promise a specific uplift or invent projections. I identify and explain conversion barriers within the agreed scope and the available evidence.

Will you rewrite my copy or redesign my pages?

The audit provides direction, priorities, and recommendations, not final copy or design files. I may suggest example wording or structural changes to illustrate a recommendation, but full copywriting, design production, and implementation are outside the audit scope.

If you need help with the redesign, see my Product Design services.

Will you implement the changes or check the results?

No. Implementation and post-change measurement are outside the audit scope. Your team can use the report to decide what to change and test.

Do you advise on product strategy, pricing, or market fit?

The audit examines how your offer and pricing are presented to users. It does not evaluate the underlying business model, set your pricing strategy, or assess product-market fit.

Payment and confidentiality

What’s your refund policy?

If the audit doesn’t meet your expectations, I’ll return your full payment, no questions asked. I stand behind the quality of my work.

How is my information kept confidential?

I sign an NDA before work begins if you request one. Information you share, including URLs, screenshots, analytics, and test accounts, is used only for your audit and kept for six months after the project is finished. I mention you or show your findings in a case study only with your permission.

Product design Service

What types of digital products can be designed?

Product design can cover websites, mobile applications, SaaS products, enterprise platforms, internal tools and AI-powered applications. The scope depends on the product's complexity, audience and business objectives.

Can an existing product be redesigned?

Yes. Product redesign can address usability issues, confusing navigation, inefficient workflows, inconsistent interfaces or changing business requirements. The work begins with understanding the current experience and identifying what needs to improve.

Can product design start from an idea?

Yes. Early-stage product design can include defining the core user journey, structuring functionality, creating wireframes and developing prototypes before full development begins.

What does a product design project include?

Deliverables may include research, information architecture, user flows, wireframes, prototypes, UI design, design system components and development specifications. The exact scope is defined according to the project.

How much do product design services cost?

The cost depends on the product's complexity, project scope, timeline and required deliverables. An estimate can be provided after the project requirements and expected outcomes are clarified.
