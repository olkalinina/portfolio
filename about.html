👋Hi, I'm Olga, a product designer who loves a hard problem.

For 20+ years I've designed complex digital products: platforms, enterprise tools and AI-driven systems where structure and clarity do the heaviest work. The harder the problem, the more I enjoy it.

I'm an expert.

I started in graphic design and grew into the structure behind products. Today I specialise in:

Complex platforms and enterprise tools

Hundreds of entity types, nested workflows, expert and first-time users. I make them navigable.

Data-heavy products

Large amounts of data, turned into interfaces people can read, trust and act on.

AI-powered products

Clear, predictable and user-controlled interactions with intelligent systems.

Behavioral CRO

Finding out why users hesitate, and what is worth improving first.

I love a challenge.

In design, and outside it.

In design

The problems I find most interesting are structural ones. How does a product handle scale? How do you design for expert users without oversimplifying, and for new users without overwhelming them?

In life

In 2023 I rode horseback for three weeks along the Great Silk Road in Kyrgyzstan, up to around 4,000 metres. It was hard and beautiful, and it taught me a patience that turns out to be useful in design too.

Always learning

I moved from visual advertising to product systems, and I keep adding new ground: AI-driven products, behavioral analysis, and whatever the next problem asks for.
