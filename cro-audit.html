CRO audit

CRO audit for websites and SaaS

People visit, but sign-ups, trials or purchases fall short. I review your key funnel, find where visitors drop off and lose trust, and give you a prioritised plan of changes worth testing.

Request an audit See examples

What I review

The pages and flows where a visitor decides to trust you or leave.

  • Landing pages
  • Pricing and plans
  • Sign-up forms
  • Onboarding
  • Checkout and upgrade
  • Error and policy messages

How I find the problems

I combine three things, so findings are grounded and not only opinion.

UX heuristics

A structured review of clarity, hierarchy, consistency and friction on every step.

Behavioural insight

Where doubt, effort and mixed signals make people hesitate, and why.

Your data

Your analytics and any recordings or research you have, to point at the steps that matter most.

What you get

  • A funnel walkthrough with the friction and trust gaps found at each step
  • Prioritised fixes, ranked by expected impact and effort
  • Wireframe-level redesign proposals and A/B test ideas for the highest-impact changes

How it works

  1. BriefYou share your goal, the funnel to look at and any analytics you can open up.
  2. ReviewI go through the journey as a new visitor, on desktop and on mobile.
  3. ReportPrioritised findings and redesign proposals, written to act on.
  4. WalkthroughWe go through the findings together and agree what to test first.
