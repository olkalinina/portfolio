Behavioral CRO Diagnosis

Why aren't users converting?

Your product may be losing potential customers for reasons that aren't immediately obvious. I investigate where users encounter friction, what undermines trust, and why your website or product isn't moving them toward the next step.

Discuss your project

What the diagnosis investigates

Depending on the project, I look at some or all of these areas. We agree on the focus at the start.

Value proposition
Whether users understand what the product offers and why it matters to them.
Trust and credibility
What helps or prevents users from trusting the product and its offer.
Conversion journey
Where obstacles emerge between the first interaction and the intended action.
Onboarding and activation
What makes it difficult for users to get started and experience the product's value.
Decision friction
What prevents users from choosing a plan, signing up, purchasing, or continuing.

Who it's for

Primarily founders and small teams building these kinds of products:

  • SaaS products
  • AI tools
  • Digital platforms
  • Marketplaces
  • E-commerce
  • Other digital services

It is particularly useful when:

  • Traffic exists, but conversion is below expectations.
  • Users fail to complete important steps or reach activation.
  • The value proposition is difficult to communicate.
  • The team knows something is not working but does not know where to focus.
  • The product has accumulated UX issues, but the team needs to understand which ones matter most.

How it works

  1. ContextUnderstand the product, target audience, business goals and current challenges.
  2. InvestigationExamine the interface and key user journey, using available evidence and analytics where appropriate.
  3. DiagnosisIdentify problems, interpret their possible behavioral causes, and explain their implications for conversion.
  4. Action planTranslate findings into prioritized recommendations, explaining what to improve and why.

Findings are labelled by the evidence behind them. What can be observed in the product is kept separate from what is a reasoned hypothesis about why users behave as they do, and conclusions are calibrated to the evidence available.

What you'll receive

  • A structured diagnosis report.
  • Evidence-backed findings, supported by annotated screenshots where useful.
  • Explanations of the behavioral implications of each identified problem.
  • Prioritized recommendations and implementation direction.
  • A practical action plan to help the team decide what to address first.
